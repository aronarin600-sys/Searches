Supabase Database Setup Guide

To activate your backend, follow these 3 simple steps:

  1. Log in to your free account at supabase.com.
  2. Open the SQL Editor tab on the left sidebar.
  3. Open the file supabase_schema.sql in your project, paste its contents into the SQL editor, and click Run.
  4. Create your administrator user in Supabase under Authentication > Users.
  5. Click Database Setup above to enter your Project URL and public "anon" key.
